Every screen you own

Haze for Mac

One window, on one sky. Your places in a column, the forecast laid out as a spread, and the radar or Settings in a panel beside it. The temperature sits in the menu bar, and there's a key for everything.

Haze for iPad

At full width the iPad gets the Mac's window: the forecast on the near page, the radar or Settings on the far one, and the Mac's keyboard commands. In Slide Over it folds back into the iPhone page, and whatever was open stays open.

Made for iPhone Duo

Opened like a book, the forecast holds one page and the sun or the radar the other, meeting at the hinge. On both screens every control stands in the column beside the status bar, with tonight's sunset stood on end at its foot, so the page never runs under a button.

Haze for Apple Watch

The watch app and its complications now arrive with the iPhone app. The phone hands the wrist the same reading it shows, and when the phone has been quiet a while, the watch fetches its own.

Accurate, and consistent about it

Numbers that agree

One reading, everywhere

The main screen, the hourly forecast, your saved places, the widgets, the watch and Siri all show the same current temperature, and the widgets keep up without the app being opened.

Checked against a real instrument

In the US the nearest weather station corrects the modeled now, so a storm overhead never reads as partly cloudy, and the page says which station it was.

Calibrated where it counts

US highs, lows and rain amounts come from the National Blend of Models, the same guidance behind the official forecast, rather than one model running hot.

Clock changes, handled

Sunrise, sunset and every hour after a daylight saving change land on the right minute, and alerts fire in the city's own time, wherever you're reading from.

Everything inside, briefly

The sky

Every screen sits on a gradient drawn from the conditions and the sun's position, deepened so white type always reads.

The daily brief

The day ahead in two or three composed sentences, written by careful templating, not an AI.

The forecast

Hourly with rain chances, a 72-hour trend you can scrub with a finger, and ten days out, each day opening hour by hour.

The radar

Observed NEXRAD frames and HRRR hours ahead in the US, RainViewer elsewhere, on a timeline you can play or scrub.

The nowcast

"Rain starting around 3:15," in plain words from 15-minute data, right under the temperature.

Live Activity

An imminent shower on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with the time it arrives and how hard it falls.

Rain alerts

With notifications on, a background check warns you before rain starts, even while the app is closed.

Advisories

National Weather Service watches and warnings in a quiet pill, never a flashing banner.

The sun

A sunrise and sunset arc you can drag across the day, golden hour times, and an optional heads-up.

Sunsets, rated

A score for how good the next sunrise or sunset will look, judged from the cloud deck the forecast is promising.

The moon

Phase and illumination, computed on the device. The moon doesn't need an API.

The share card

Today's forecast composed as a 4:5 card, the sky and the serif temperature, ready to send.

The details

Wind compass, UV, humidity, pressure, cloud cover, dew point and precipitation, each opening into a scrubbable chart.

Air quality

The US Air Quality Index, with PM2.5, PM10, ozone and nitrogen dioxide behind it.

Widgets

Home Screen small, medium and large, and the Lock Screen, each pinnable to any saved city, and current on their own.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask for the weather and Siri answers from the last forecast, saying how recent it is. Opening the radar is a shortcut too.

The morning digest

The daily brief as a notification, at a time you choose.

Two voices

Every sentence the app composes is written twice: plainly, or with a lighter step. Whimsy mode picks the second.

App icons

Four icons, one per sky: day, dusk, night and dawn.

Units and settings

Fahrenheit or Celsius, mph or km/h, 12 or 24 hour time, text size, and which cards appear on the main screen.

Accessibility

VoiceOver reads every number in context, and Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency actually change the rendering.

Search

Any city or airport in the world, saved in a list that stays in your order.
