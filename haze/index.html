Haze

Beautiful, accurate weather

A weather app set like a magazine, not a dashboard. Big serif numerals, soft skies that shift with the time of day, and forecasts you can rely on. No ads, no accounts.

Download on the App Store

Free · iPhone, iOS 18 · Apple Watch

The same app at noon, in a heat wave, at dusk and past midnight.

The sky

Every screen sits on a gradient drawn from the conditions and the sun's position, deepened so white type always reads.

The forecast

Hourly, a 72-hour trend you can scrub with a finger, and ten days out, each day opening hour by hour.

The radar

Observed NEXRAD frames and HRRR hours ahead in the US, RainViewer elsewhere, on a timeline you can play or scrub.

Rain

A plain-words nowcast from 15-minute data, a Live Activity while a shower passes, and alerts while the app is closed.

Advisories

National Weather Service watches and warnings in a quiet pill, never a flashing banner.

The sun and the moon

A draggable sun arc, sunrises and sunsets rated from the cloud deck, golden hour times, and the moon's phase computed on device.

The details

Wind, UV, humidity, pressure, cloud cover, dew point, precipitation and US air quality, each opening into a chart.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen, small to large, pinnable to any saved city.

Apple Watch

An app for the wrist and complications in four families, fed by the phone or fetching on their own.

Yours

Saved places sync through iCloud. Siri answers from the last forecast, even offline. Four app icons, one per sky. A morning digest at a time you choose.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

Forecasts blend the ECMWF, GFS and ICON models through Open-Meteo, and in the US the nearest weather station corrects the now. The code is public at github.com/ankthba/haze.
