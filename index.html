Hey, I’m Aniketh, studying CS and Applied Math at the University of Virginia.

Right now I’m building Pragmatic, the system of record for AI agents, and OCRadar, which uses AI to help catch oral cancer early. I also built Haze, a 5-star rated weather app for iPhone. My work has reached millions of people worldwide and been covered by WTOP, Korea Times, United Airlines, and Washington Dulles International Airport among others.

In a previous life, I was an engineering intern at Alarm.com, led software at Oakton Robotics for four years, attended Y Combinator Startup School, and spoke at the Tech Roast Show. Outside work, I photograph aircraft at Dulles and landscapes around the world. I also hike and listen to a lot of music.
