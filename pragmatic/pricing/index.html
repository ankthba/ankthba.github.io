When you actually pay.

The runtime is source-available under the Business Source License 1.1: read it, modify it, run it. It's free for non-production use, and free in production for organizations under $1M in annual revenue, as long as you're not reselling it to others as a hosted service. Past that, production use needs a commercial license. Every released version turns into Apache 2.0 four years after it ships, so nothing is ever locked up for good.

Common questions

Is it free to try?

›
Yes. The entire runtime is free for non-production use, with no license key. It isn't publicly downloadable yet, so request early access and we'll get you the source and set you up.

When do I need to pay?

›
When you run Pragmatic in production and your organization makes over $1M in annual revenue, or when you offer it to third parties as a hosted or managed service. Both cases need a commercial license.

What do the paid plans add?

›
The commercial license itself, plus hosted journals, a shared team console, alerting, and support. The runtime is identical. You're paying for production rights and the operational layer around it, not for a different engine.

What happens at the Change Date?

›
Four years after a version ships, that version automatically relicenses to Apache 2.0, fully open source with no restrictions. The Business Source License is a delay on openness, not a permanent enclosure.
