Aniketh Bandlamudi

I study computer science at the University of Virginia, minoring in applied mathematics, and plan to minor in philosophy too. Right now I’m building Pragmatic, the system of record for AI agents, and OCRadar, which uses AI to help catch oral cancer early. I started OCRadar in eighth grade, and it’s since been covered by WTOP, the Korea Times and others. I’ve also published research on using neural networks to detect oral cancer. I also built Haze, a weather app for iPhone. In my free time, I build fun projects.

Before UVA, I led software for Oakton’s robotics teams for four years. I’ve also interned at Alarm.com, attended Y Combinator’s AI Startup School, and spoken at the Tech Roast Show.

Outside work, I’m an aviation and landscape photographer. My photos have been featured by more than a dozen airlines and airports, including United, Dulles and Lufthansa, with special recognition from Korean Air and BLACKPINK. I also write, and I’m usually outdoors with music playing.
