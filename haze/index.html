Haze

Beautiful, accurate weather

A weather app set like a magazine, not a dashboard. Big serif numerals, soft skies that shift with the time of day, and forecasts you can rely on. No ads, no accounts.

Download on the App Store

Free · iPhone, iOS 18 · Apple Watch

The sky in the app follows the time of day.

Everything inside, briefly.

The sky

Every screen sits on a gradient drawn from the conditions and the sun's position, deepened so white type always reads.

The daily brief

The day ahead in two or three composed sentences, written by careful templating, not an AI.

The forecast

Hourly with rain chances, a 72-hour trend you can scrub with a finger, and ten days out, each day opening hour by hour.

The radar

Observed NEXRAD frames and HRRR hours ahead in the US, RainViewer elsewhere, on a timeline you can play or scrub.

The nowcast

"Rain starting around 3:15," in plain words from 15-minute data, right under the temperature.

Live Activity

An imminent shower on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, its intensity drawn as a curve on a real time axis.

Rain alerts

With notifications on, a background check warns you before rain starts, even while the app is closed.

Advisories

National Weather Service watches and warnings in a quiet pill, never a flashing banner.

The station check

In the US, the nearest weather station corrects the modeled now, so a storm overhead never reads as partly cloudy.

The sun

A sunrise and sunset arc you can drag across the day, and golden hour times with an optional heads-up.

Sunsets, rated

A score for how good the next sunrise or sunset will look, judged from the cloud deck the forecast is promising.

The moon

Phase and illumination, computed on the device. The moon doesn't need an API.

The details

Wind compass, UV, humidity, pressure, cloud cover, dew point and precipitation, each opening into a scrubbable chart.

Air quality

The US Air Quality Index, with PM2.5, PM10, ozone and nitrogen dioxide behind it.

Widgets

Home Screen small, medium and large, Lock Screen inline, circular and rectangular, each pinnable to any saved city.

Apple Watch

An app for the wrist and complications in four families, fed by the phone or fetching on their own.

Siri and Shortcuts

Ask for the weather and Siri answers from the last forecast, instantly, even offline. Opening the radar is a shortcut too.

The morning digest

The daily brief as a notification, at a time you choose.

The share card

Today's forecast composed as a 4:5 card, the sky and the serif temperature, rendered as a PNG when you share it.

App icons

Four icons, one per sky: day, dusk, night and dawn.

Search

Any city in the world, with full state names in the US, saved in a list that stays in your order.

iCloud sync

Saved places and settings follow you to a new phone. No account of mine, just yours.

Units and settings

Fahrenheit or Celsius, mph or km/h, 12 or 24 hour time, text size, haptics, even which cards appear on the main screen.

Accessibility

Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and Reduce Transparency actually change the rendering.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

Forecasts blend the ECMWF, GFS and ICON models through Open-Meteo.
