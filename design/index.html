About

Lagunita is a variable Garamond for screens, built from EB Garamond. It takes the flared stems, chisel-cut terminals and taller t of the Stanford wordmark. One optical-size axis runs from a sturdy Text cut for reading to a sharp, high-contrast Display cut for headlines, in weights from Light to ExtraBold, roman and italic. It is free under the SIL Open Font License.

Haze is a weather app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, built solo in Swift, with big serif numerals and skies that shift with the day. This website is set in Newsreader and Lagunita, inside coloured-pencil drawings.
