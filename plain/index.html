Aniketh Bandlamudi

I’m building Pragmatic, the system of record for AI agents. Its Rust runtime records every model and tool call an agent makes, so any run can be replayed exactly, a guarantee I’ve proved in Lean 4. I also lead OCRadar, the nonprofit I founded in eighth grade to catch oral cancer early. Our neural network is 98% accurate in testing and runs entirely on a phone, and the work has been covered by WTOP, the Korea Times and others.

I study computer science, applied math and philosophy at the University of Virginia. Before UVA, I led software for Oakton’s robotics teams for four years, interned at Alarm.com, attended Y Combinator’s AI Startup School, and spoke at the Tech Roast Show. I’ve also built Haze, a weather app for iPhone, a simulation of all 144,209 neurons in a fruit fly’s brain that tests whether memories replay during sleep, and plenty more.

Outside of work, I love art and art history. I’m an aviation and landscape photographer, and more than a dozen airlines and airports have featured my photos, including United, Dulles and Lufthansa, with special recognition from Korean Air and BLACKPINK. I also write, and I’m usually outdoors with music playing.
